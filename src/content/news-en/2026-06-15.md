---
title: "A well-deserved rest – and the first numbers on the website"
date: 2026-06-15
---

Saturday 13 June was a demanding afternoon: after the efforts of the concert, we decided we deserve a little rest. Rehearsals are therefore suspended for Thursday 19 June and will resume as usual on Thursday 25 with our customary enthusiasm for making music together.

In the [**Gallery**](/en/galleria) section of the website you can find photos and videos from the concert. We are particularly pleased with the videos, which allow us — not something to be taken for granted for those who play — to listen from the outside to what we have done: a valuable way to understand what works and what we can improve. A heartfelt thank you to everyone who sent us their material: every contribution is welcome and helps build the visual and audio memory of our activities. We remind you that the website is open to anyone who wishes to share photos or videos of our events: you can contact us by writing to [**info@turriaco.it**](mailto:info@turriaco.it).

After the website's launch last Saturday, we looked with curiosity at the first numbers. On Saturday and Sunday the website received 54 visits, for a total of 259 pages viewed: for a debut, we cannot complain.

One clarification worth making: under the [**Privacy Policy**](/en/privacy) we have chosen to adopt, we are unable to tell you how many distinct individuals these visits came from. This is not a technical limitation, but a deliberate choice. The analytics system we use does not profile individual users and does not collect personal data: the information available is exclusively aggregated. We preferred this approach to minimise the legal and administrative obligations related to running the website, while respecting the privacy of those who visit us.

Thank you all for the interest and warmth shown from the very first day. And, for the band members — see you on the 25th!
