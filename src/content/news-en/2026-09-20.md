On Sunday, September 20th, as part of Cormons' local Grape Festival, the gathering of the bands belonging to the Gorizia Provincial Delegation of ANBIMA (National Association of Autonomous Italian Musical Bands) will take place in Cormons. The band parade will begin at 3:30 p.m.
